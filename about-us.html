ABOUT US LATO MILK UGANDA LTD

The foundation of a happy, healthy lifestyle

Who We Are

Lato is the consumer brand of Pearl Dairy Farms Limited, a Ugandan dairy company with its head office and main factory at Biharwe in Mbarara. Our milk comes straight from the land of milk and honey, from local farmers who deliver fresh cow's milk to our plant.

From instant milk powders and UHT milk to yoghurt, butter, ghee, fortified child nutrition and Nadolac infant cereals, every product is made to keep the goodness of milk intact and to be pure, tasty and nutritious for families.

Our Purpose and Philosophy

Our Purpose

To improve the livelihoods of smallholder farmers across East Africa while helping build a strong, resilient next generation by meeting the nutrition needs of today's children. We aim to become the region's leading nutrition company, offering high-quality products, driving innovation and helping to reduce malnutrition.

Our Philosophy

Our actions are driven by success and a deep desire to serve our customers. We set out to give local dairy farmers in Mbarara a place to process their produce, and we grow a vibrant dairy industry on integrity, speed and character, so the business stays scalable, sustainable and profitable.

Our Vision and Mission

Vision

To be a leading, benchmark maker and supplier of safe, high-quality dairy products across Africa and around the world.

Mission

To produce and market milk and dairy products that meet international standards, using modern manufacturing technology, high hygiene standards and smart marketing at competitive prices, so that everyone we serve is nourished and delighted.
